Every display on this page ships in the builder two ways: (1) as a template for a whole page/site dedicated to displaying images that way, and (2) as a block anyone can drop into the main homepage or any page they build. Built as working samples for the JV intranet — these 10 are the "effects" family (CD1–CD10), alongside the A1–A10 structural displays in the Options Galleries. Honest builder notes: #3 tilt, #5 cursor trail and #6 orbit are garnish, not staples — lovely on a hero or a feature page, tiring if every page does it. #1, #2-style masonry, #7 lightbox and #8 accordion are the workhorses. Anything hover-based needs a tap equivalent on phones, per the device-visibility rules (v7).
