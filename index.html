Put the whole site into edit mode: every editable button, card and section gets a dotted outline — click to select, drag to move things around, add new things in place. Changes publish through the same draft → preview → publish flow, audit-logged.

Holiday themes can be scheduled — set the December theme in October and it switches itself on Dec 1 and off Jan 2.

You choose what lives on the rail: add or remove items, switch them off without deleting, move them up and down (first row, second row…), add sub-headers with items under them, and pick each item's icon. The rail rebuilds the moment you change anything here.
